FACTS (verified 2026-11-14 ~2:30 PM CST):
- Nearly 10,000 flights cancelled by 11:30 PM Nov 13 (Flightradar24 via Reuters). Ground stop >4h; longest since 9/11. Reuters: https://www.reuters.com/3fec1b0a85a0/world/us/faa-issues-national-ground-stop-due-extraordinary-circumstance-reasons-safety-2026-11-13/
- Official cause (Duffy, DOT ICYMI): contractor L3Harris doing "routine maintenance" at "at least one" remote FAA site, switching copper to fiber; a router flooded the ATC network (>1,000 connection attempts every 4–5 s). "Once we were able to isolate the work ... all of our systems came back online." https://www.transportation.gov/briefing-room/icymi-us-transportation-secretary-sean-p-duffy-provides-update-restoration-airspace
- Funding: earlier Friday, before the outage, White House/DOT urged Congress to approve $30.1B; after, Duffy called for $50B (Reuters).
- DHS Sec. Mullin: "zero evidence" of a cyberattack so far; not ruled out; investigation ongoing (Reuters, DHS).
- Refund: DOT — refund owed if the airline cancels for any reason and you decline rebooking/vouchers; 7 business days (card) / 20 days (other). 14 CFR 260.6; https://www.transportation.gov/individuals/aviation-consumer-protection/refunds
- Reuters: airlines not required to pay extra compensation for this outage (refund ≠ compensation). Video says "refund" only.
