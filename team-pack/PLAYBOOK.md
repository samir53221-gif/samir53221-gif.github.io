## 1. The goal (الهدف)

1. **Money.** Path: audience → platform monetization → our own products.
   - YouTube YPP: 500 subs + 3M Shorts views / 90 days = fan funding only; ad revenue needs 1,000 subs + 10M Shorts views / 90 days.
   - TikTok Creator Rewards: 10,000 followers + 100,000 views / 30 days, videos ≥ 1 minute.
   - Faster money before thresholds: comment-keyword funnel to the free starter checklist → LandlordLedger Pro / $9 Rent Ledger (Gumroad: https://samirwave41.gumroad.com/l/jtdey), affiliates, sponsorships once views are steady. Tamara/SIBAQ later, separate pages.
2. **Why trends:** a hot trend brings people who are already searching for that subject. We don't have to buy reach; the platform pushes the topic. Our job: be early, be the best-looking "pick one" on that topic, and make people argue in comments (comments + watch time = more distribution).
3. **Per-video target:** 1,000+ views, watch > 50%, real comments. Comments are the main signal.

## 2. The winning format (الفورمات اللي كسبت)

- **Choice / competition, pick ONE.** "Who wins?", "Pick one", "PASS or FAIL — you're the jury". Neutral: we never hint at a winner or mock anyone. The voice itself asks the question.
- **Pure trend.** No real-estate tie-in: no property prices, no real-estate captions or hashtags. (GTA "Florida prices" and BookTok #realestateinvesting both underperformed.)
- **Trend subject huge from second 1.** First frame = the trend's main person/thing (e.g. Pattinson for Bond, RDJ/Doom for Doomsday) + trend name + key hashtag + what is new ("final trailer just dropped", "opened to $56M"). Hook fact first, question in the first 3 seconds — never at the end only.
- **The trend owner stays at the center** of every follow-up episode (Bond Round 2 without Pattinson = rejected).
- **Real actor photos**, licensed (Wikimedia Commons), **AI-enhanced for sharpness/light/color without changing the face**. Each actor labelled by name (and role). Never AI-morphed or regenerated faces.
- **Hero spotlight reveal, one-by-one** (tuxedo / BookTok shelf style): one hero big in the center, others blurred behind; hero gets a moment (name + power/claim), goes back, next one comes forward. Never a side-by-side grid of photos.
- **Battle / clash sequence** for versus content: the sides face off (energy hits, impacts, camera shake, color-coded sides) BEFORE the final choice screen.
- **Final choice screen:** the options split cleanly, the voice says "No mixing. Who wins? Comment X or Y." Proper ending that loops back to the start (never feels cut off).
- **Length:** ~25–35 s for pick-one trend videos (TikTok Creator Rewards needs ≥ 60 s; test a 60 s version only when a format is already winning).

## 3. Sound (الصوت)

- **Narrator/host VO is mandatory.** Approved voices: Kokoro **am_michael** (male) / **af_heart** (female), studio processing, speed **0.86–0.92**, calm premium tone. VO presents the choices and asks viewers to comment their pick.
- **Music low under the voice** (ducked), royalty-free/CC only (e.g. Kevin MacLeod CC BY 4.0, credited in caption). No copyrighted songs. Copy the *style* of the million-view reference's music.
- **SFX:** whooshes, impacts, risers on cuts. Never music-only. Never silent.

## 4. Visuals (الصورة)

- Bright, clean, high-contrast. **No dark/gloomy grading.** Faces must be readable on a phone.
- Motion every 0.5–1 s: punch-ins, whip pans, beat cuts, kinetic text (2–4 words, bold, lower third, never over faces).
- The featured object/person is the hero; layout balanced, nothing crammed.
- Real looking: real photos / official footage with credit / news style. **No cartoon, no Xbox/GTA-looking AI characters, no static slideshow.**

## 5. Caption & posting (الكابشن والنشر)

Caption must contain, in this order:
1. Hook line with the trend news (what just happened + date).
2. The choices, one line each, with a one-line case for each.
3. The call: "No mixing. Who wins? Comment ___ or ___."
4. Links: official trailer, official movie/product page, tickets/where to get it (when they exist).
5. Credits: footage/photo licenses, music credit, "not affiliated" line.
6. Hashtags: trend tags + one tag per featured actor/character + the hashtags used by the million-view references on that trend, **tailored per platform** (TikTok / YouTube / Instagram / Facebook).
- Title (YouTube) = hook + choice + #Shorts.
- **Pinned comment** with the choice ("No mixing. Pick one: A, B or C.") on every platform — Ahmed pins manually if the tool can't.
- Platforms: TikTok + YouTube + Instagram (when not suspended) + Facebook (only if a page is connected in Buffer).
- **Publish immediately** once Ahmed approves the final video — the trend doesn't wait for midnight.
- Hosting: samir53221-gif.github.io (no Google Drive).

## 6. Comments & funnel (الكومنتات)

- Replies: funny, human, Egyptian-style humor translated to natural English, **no questions**, never sounds like AI, never promises ("winner gets the tux next episode" is banned).
- Reply to every comment (rounds every 3 h, 10 AM–10 PM CST) incl. nested replies.
- Subscribe/follow calls stay classy and indirect: free value → comment keyword for a freebie → bigger freebies for followers. Never "subscribe to get the answer".
- Comment votes feed the next episode idea (e.g. Cavill vs Elba).

## 7. The process — every single video (الخطوات)

1. Read this PLAYBOOK.md.
2. Verify the trend is real on the platform (views/time from a public source) and find 2–3 million-view references on that trend (hook, music style, hashtags, length).
3. **Send Ahmed the plan first** (30-second shot list + VO lines + caption outline). Wait for OK. ("قبل ما تعمل أي حاجة، بلغني.")
4. Make **one sample**: one enhanced photo (before/after) + one 3-second battle/hero shot. Wait for OK.
5. Build the full video from the approved template (Section 9) — change the subject, not the system.
6. Run the **QA checklist** (Section 8). Fix until every box passes.
7. Preview to Ahmed: video + caption (copy-paste text in its own message) + pinned comment.
8. On OK → publish now on all connected platforms → send links.
9. Metrics check at +3 h, +24 h, +72 h; log in the results table (Section 10) with the reason it won/failed.

## 8. Mandatory pre-preview QA checklist (لازم كله ✓ قبل ما أحمد يشوف)

- [ ] **VO present** in the final file: audio stream exists; loudness check shows speech (not just music); listen to the full file start to end.
- [ ] **VO correlation check**: the final mix's audio matches the approved VO track (cross-correlation / waveform check) — catches the Hunger Games bug where the VO was dropped in the mix.
- [ ] Music audibly lower than voice; SFX on cuts.
- [ ] **Brightness**: no frame is dark/muddy (average luma check on sampled frames); colors vivid.
- [ ] **Faces readable** at phone size; likeness unchanged after enhancement; every actor labelled.
- [ ] Trend subject + trend name + hashtag visible in second 1; question asked in first 3 s.
- [ ] One-by-one hero reveal (no side-by-side grids); battle sequence present for versus videos.
- [ ] Layout balanced; text not covering faces; safe zones for TikTok/YouTube UI.
- [ ] No static stretch > 1 s; proper ending/loop.
- [ ] No real-estate words/hashtags anywhere.
- [ ] Caption complete: hook, choices, call, trailer/movie/tickets links, credits, per-actor + trend hashtags per platform; pinned comment text ready.
- [ ] Contact sheet + 3 stills reviewed against the reference videos side-by-side.

## 11. Rejected approaches — never again (ممنوع نرجع لها)

- Dark, gloomy, low-contrast video (Avengers v1, Nov 23).
- Cartoon / Xbox / GTA-looking AI characters instead of real actors (Avengers v2; BUNKER).
- Side-by-side photo grids; photos placed next to each other with no reveal (Avengers v3).
- Static slideshow / still photo with light zoom.
- Music-only or silent upload (Hunger Games Nov 22; first Elevator video).
- AI-morphed/regenerated celebrity faces that don't look like the actor.
- Real-estate tie-ins in trend videos (GTA Florida prices, BookTok #realestateinvesting).
- Question only at the end (GTA dialog videos: 0 comments, people left at ~2 s).
- Follow-up episode without the trend's main subject (Bond Round 2 without Pattinson).
- Long talking-head dialogs (42–44 s man/woman business ideas: 0–76 views).
- Fast robotic VO (3.3–3.5 words/s).
- Promises in replies or captions ("winner gets the tux next episode").
- Questions in comment replies; AI-sounding replies.
- Holding a hot trend video until midnight.
- Rumor hashtags (e.g. #faagroundstop was removed by TikTok).
